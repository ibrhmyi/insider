# Gate 1 feasibility check (draft episode definition)

Generated by `src/feasibility_gate1.py`. Feasibility only: counts, prices at alert and power. **No outcome was looked at**: no won/lost flag, outcome price, payout, PnL or win rate was loaded, joined, printed or computed. Outcome columns are dropped at load (`after_resolution, excess, outcomePrices, outcome_prices, payout, pnl, prior_excess, resolution, won, yes_px_at_cutoff, yes_px_at_dec, yes_won`). Resolution *dates* are used only to assign the period.

## 0. Read this first: what is exact and what is a proxy

- **v1 markets (Maduro, Nobel, Iran): exact.** Every maker and taker fill is in the repo (`data/clean/v1_fills_*.csv`). T_dec = the first public news time in `data/markets.csv` (the v1 decision time).
- **v2 markets (165): PROXY.** 0 of 165 have fills in this container. The v2 fill cache `data/raw/v2_fills/` is git-ignored and was never committed, so it is not in this container. The brief says fetch nothing new, so nothing was re-fetched. For those markets the episode counts below come from `data/v2/positions.csv.gz` (wallet x market x side aggregates up to T_dec). That proxy (a) only sees wallets still net long at close (244,312 sold-out units are missing), (b) only counts a wallet whose pre-T_dec buys all fall inside 24 h with no sells, (c) sets t_a to the wallet's last pre-T_dec buy (the true t_a can only be earlier) and (d) sets p_a to the wallet's own buy VWAP, because the market price in the 10 minutes after t_a needs the fills. **The proxy undercounts episodes and wallets, and its p_a is not the specified p_a.** Rerunning this script after re-pulling the 165 fill files gives the exact numbers without any code change. Section 5 gives the time estimate.
- Main definition: $2,500 of net buys within 24 h at a buy VWAP <= 0.35. Variants: $1,000 and $10,000 at a cap of 0.35, and $2,500 at a cap of 0.20. Every count is shown twice: episodes with t_a < T_dec, and episodes with t_a < T_dec - 24 h.
- Definition choices made here (the draft did not fix them): net buys = buy USD minus sell USD of the same token by the same wallet in the window. VWAP = the wallet's buy VWAP in the window. A trigger is the buy fill where the condition first becomes true (it must turn false and then true again to trigger again). Episodes chain: same side, consecutive triggers <= 24 h apart. p_a uses fills strictly after t_a; 'last fill before t_a' means strictly earlier; if there is none, the trigger fill's price is used.

## 4. Power from p_a only (no outcomes)

Excess per episode = outcome - p_a; under no edge its variance is p_a(1 - p_a). Market-level mean excess = mean over the market's episodes; cell estimate = mean over markets with at least one episode. Two SEs: **independent** treats episodes in a market as independent (variance of the market mean = sum p(1-p) / n^2), as the brief specifies; **correlated** treats them as fully dependent, because all same-side episodes in a market share one outcome (variance = mean p(1-p)). The truth lies between and is near the correlated value when a market's episodes are on one side. MDE = 2.8 x SE (5% two-sided, 80% power). v2 rows use the proxy p_a where the fills are missing; 'all' pools v2 and v1.
