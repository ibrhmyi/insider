# Gate 1 feasibility check (draft episode definition), v2: exact fills, larger universe

Generated by `src/feasibility_gate1.py`. Feasibility only: counts, prices at alert and power. **No outcome was looked at**: no won/lost flag, outcome price, payout, PnL or win rate was loaded, joined, printed or computed. Outcome columns are dropped at load (`after_resolution, excess, outcomePrices, outcome_prices, payout, pnl, prior_excess, resolution, won, yes_px_at_cutoff, yes_px_at_dec, yes_won`). Resolution *dates* are used only to assign the period. Gate 1 itself was not run.

## 0. What changed since the first version

- **Exact fills everywhere; no proxy.** All maker and taker fills were fetched from `/v2/trades` into the local cache (see `data/v2/fills_manifest.csv`; rebuild with `python src/v3_01_fetch.py --manifest`). The episode code is unchanged.
- **Universe ('core', 2,059 markets fetched or attempted):** the 165 v2 markets; every military/government market in v2's ranked list that v2 had not fetched (1,835); the 58 military/government markets over $12M that Amendment B excluded (no page cap); the v1 Maduro, Nobel and Iran markets. **FFIC markets** (32) are reported in their own block (section 6) and enter the core tables only if they are also in the core set. Each market is counted once: the first version counted Nobel twice (it is both a v1 market and one of the 165).
- **Exclusions.** A market is excluded if T_dec is within 24 h of its first fill (no usable pre-decision period), if the fetch failed or hit the 400-page cap (v2 rule sets only), or if it has no fills before the cutoff. 238 core markets are excluded; reasons in section 1.
- **Event unit.** Every market has Gamma's `event_id`. Power is reported with the market as the unit and with the event as the unit (episodes pooled within event, then averaged across events). Gamma often puts 'by Friday' and 'by Sunday' markets in *different* events that share a Gamma *series*. A series-level row (series id where Gamma has one, else the event) is added as a sensitivity check.
- T_dec: price-path rule from v2 (the rolling median Yes price leaves 0.05 to 0.95 for good, before resolution minus 1 h). v1 markets use the first news time.
- Definition details, unchanged from the first version: net buys = buy USD minus sell USD of the same token by the same wallet in the 24 h window. VWAP = the wallet's buy VWAP in that window. A trigger is a rising edge at a buy fill. Episodes chain same-side triggers that are <= 24 h apart. p_a is taken from fills strictly after t_a, then the last fill strictly before t_a, then the trigger fill.

## 4. Power from p_a only (no outcomes)

Excess per episode = outcome - p_a; under no edge its variance is p_a(1 - p_a). The unit's mean excess = the mean over its episodes; the cell estimate = the mean over units with at least one episode. MDE = 2.8 x SE (5% two-sided, 80% power). **indep.** treats a unit's episodes as independent (variance of the unit mean = sum p(1-p) / n^2). **corr.** treats them as fully dependent (variance = mean p(1-p)), because same-side episodes in one market share an outcome and markets in one event are linked. The truth lies between the two, near corr. for events. Market unit = each market. Event unit = Gamma event id: all episodes of the event's markets in the cell are pooled, then averaged across events. Series = Gamma series where there is one (else the event), shown for the pooled row only (n/a in category rows).
